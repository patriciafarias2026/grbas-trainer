GRBAS Trainer

296 voices · perceptual calibration vs. consensus

An interactive training tool built on the Perceptual Voice Qualities Database (PVQD). Rate any of the 296 voices on Grade, Roughness, Breathiness, Asthenia and Strain, compare your ratings against the dataset consensus, and track your calibration over time — with weighted kappa, per-parameter error, and a training-evolution chart.

Concept and clinical direction: Dr. Patricia G. Farías, Speech-Language Pathologist — Vocology Specialist, Buenos Aires, Argentina.
Built with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic).

  • Blind mode — hides the consensus grade and diagnosis until you rate, to avoid biasing perception
  • Per-parameter error, % exact match, and quadratic weighted kappa vs. consensus
  • Agreement against each individual PVQD rater
  • Rolling-window training-evolution chart
  • JSON / CSV export of your ratings
  • Runs entirely client-side — no install, no server, no data leaves your device
How it works: the tool contains the PVQD ratings, but not the audio files. Download the 296 .wav files once from the PVQD page on Mendeley Data (link below, free, CC BY 4.0) and load each voice from your device as you train. Your ratings stay in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Works offline once the page is open.

Cómo funciona: la herramienta incluye las calificaciones del PVQD, pero no los audios. Descargá una sola vez los 296 archivos .wav desde la página del PVQD en Mendeley Data (enlace abajo, gratis, CC BY 4.0) y cargá cada voz desde tu dispositivo al entrenar. Tus calificaciones quedan en tu navegador; no se sube nada.

Scope / Alcance: educational training tool, not a clinical diagnostic instrument / herramienta educativa, no diagnóstica.
